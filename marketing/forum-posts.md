## Quora (Answer template for "Can Americans visit China without a visa?")

**Answer:**

Yes — if you're transiting through China to a third country.

China's 240-hour visa-free transit policy allows US citizens (and 54 other nationalities) to stay up to 10 days without a visa. Here's how it works:

**Requirements:**
1. You must be a citizen of one of 55 eligible countries
2. You must be traveling FROM one country THROUGH China TO a different country
3. You must enter and exit at approved ports (most major airports qualify)
4. You stay within the allowed provinces for your entry point

**Example:** Flying from New York to Tokyo with a stop in Shanghai. You can leave Shanghai airport, explore Shanghai and nearby cities (Suzhou, Hangzhou, Nanjing) for up to 10 days, then continue to Tokyo.

**What you can't do:**
- Enter from Japan and exit to Japan (must be different countries)
- Visit provinces outside your allowed zone
- Stay longer than 240 hours

I built a free eligibility checker at chinatransit.info/check — it tells you instantly if your specific route qualifies.

The site also has guides for setting up mobile payments (cash is nearly dead in China), getting internet access via eSIM, and which apps to install before you go.
